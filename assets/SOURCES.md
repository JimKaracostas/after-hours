# Book cover sources

These six real, flat book covers were retrieved on 2026-09-14 from the [Open Library Covers API](https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/covers) and visually checked against their titles and authors. They are bundled to keep the local prototype reliable when offline. Cover artwork remains the property of its respective copyright holders.

The `?default=false` option makes a missing cover return an error instead of a blank placeholder. For a public deployment, follow Open Library's guidance to display covers from its hosted URLs and include a courtesy link to Open Library.
